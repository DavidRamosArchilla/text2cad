# Synthetic parts (`synth`)

Millimetre-scale mechanical parts generated by our own code ([[data.synth]]), each with an
exact CadQuery script and two descriptions. Used as training data, as GRPO prompts, and —
the held-out split — as the [[Synth benchmark]].

## Why
The public corpora are DeepCAD sketch-and-extrude parts in normalised units with
machine-written text ([[DeepCAD and Text2CAD]]). The target benchmarks describe real
parts in millimetres with holes, fillets, revolves, lofts and sweeps, in the words of an
engineer. Nothing public fills that gap with ground truth, so the generator does.

## The 17 families
| Family | Parts |
|---|---|
| `plate_holes` | rectangular plate with a hole pattern (grid / corners), optional corner fillets or chamfer |
| `flange` | round flange: disc, bolt circle, optional hub, central bore |
| `stepped_shaft` | 2–4 coaxial cylinders, optional through-bore and end chamfers |
| `polygon_prism` | triangular / pentagonal / hexagonal / octagonal prism (nut-like) with bore |
| `l_bracket` | L-shaped angle bracket with holes in both legs |
| `beam_profile` | U-channel, T-section or I-beam extruded along an axis |
| `open_box` | open-top box / enclosure made with `shell` |
| `tube_flange` | plain tube or flanged bushing |
| `slotted_block` | block with groove, pocket or obround slots |
| `cbore_plate` | plate with counterbored or countersunk holes |
| `revolved` | truncated cone, V-groove pulley, round-nosed pin (made with `revolve`) |
| `loft` | rectangle-to-circle transition piece, hopper (made with `loft`) |
| `swept_elbow` | solid bar or pipe bent 90° (made with `sweep`) |
| `gusset_bracket` | L-bracket with triangular ribs |
| `polar_pattern` | notched disc or spoked wheel (polar arrays) |
| `boss_plate` | base plate with a cylindrical boss and hole |
| `wedge` | wedge or ramp block |

## Two descriptions per part
Same split as [[Text2CAD-Bench]]: **geo** describes the finished shape; **seq** gives the
modelling recipe. Both state every key dimension, the axis it runs along and where the
origin is (scoring has no rotation alignment, so orientation must be recoverable).

> **geo** — All dimensions are in millimeters; X, Y and Z are the global coordinate axes.
> This is a circular flange whose axis is the Z-axis. The disc has an outer diameter of
> 70.0 mm and a thickness of 9.0 mm; its bottom face lies on the XY plane (Z = 0)… 6 bolt
> holes with a diameter of 8.0 mm are equally spaced on a bolt circle with a diameter of
> 55.0 mm…
>
> **seq** — Start a new part in millimeters… On the XY plane, draw a circle with a radius
> of 35mm centered at the origin and extrude it 9mm in +Z. On the top face, place 6 circles
> with a radius of 4mm evenly spaced (60 degrees apart) on a pitch circle with a radius of
> 27.5mm…

```python
import cadquery as cq
import math

flange_d = 70
flange_t = 9
bolt_circle_d = 55
bolt_d = 8
...
result = cq.Workplane("XY").circle(flange_d / 2).extrude(flange_t)
pts = [(bolt_circle_d / 2 * math.cos(math.radians(i * 60)), ...) for i in range(6)]
result = result.faces(">Z").workplane().pushPoints(pts).circle(bolt_d / 2).cutThruAll()
```

## Guarantees
Every part is executed and kept only if it is one watertight solid whose bounding box
(and, when known, volume) equals what the generator intended. Train / val / test are
disjoint by script. Fully reproducible from the seed. Yield ≈ 100 % for every family.

## Sizes (default `t2c data synth`)
20 000 train / 500 val / 400 test parts → 40 000 / 1 000 / 800 rows (geo + seq).

## Limitations
- The text is **templated** (a few phrasings per sentence, varied units and number
  formats); real prompts are freer. Paraphrasing with an LLM is a natural next step.
- 17 families cover Text2CAD-Bench L1–L2 and a few L3 operations, not free-form shapes.
- Because train and test come from the same templates, scores on the [[Synth benchmark]]
  saturate; `sft_v1_heldout.yaml` keeps three families out of training to measure transfer.
